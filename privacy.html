1. Operating entity and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Radar 36 Inc., a corporation registered in the State of Delaware, United States (8 The Green, Ste. R, City of Dover, County of Kent, DE 19901, USA), handles personal data and customer data in connection with our website, the Radar 36 security orchestration platform ("Platform") and related services.

Radar 36 is a business-to-business product for security firms and in-house security teams. It is not directed at consumers.

2. Our role: controller and processor

  • Radar 36 as controller. We decide how to handle the personal data of people who visit our website, book a demo, contact us or hold an account with us as a customer contact.
  • Customer as controller, Radar 36 as processor. Our Customers decide what they test and what data they put into the Platform. Where we process that data on a Customer’s behalf, for example in a hosted service or a support case, we act only on the Customer’s instructions.

3. Self-hosted deployments: where your security data lives

The Platform is offered primarily as self-hosted software. In a self-hosted deployment, your security testing data, including targets, credentials, scripts, workflows, logs, artifacts and findings, is stored and processed in the environment you control. Radar 36 does not have access to it.

We receive that data only if you choose to send it to us, for example by attaching logs to a support request, or if you use a hosted service that we operate for you.

4. Data we collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Business contact and account data: name, work email address, phone number, company name, role, and billing and contract records.
  • Enquiries and demo bookings: the details you give us when you email us or book a walkthrough. Bookings are handled through a third-party scheduling service, which processes the details you enter under its own privacy policy.
  • Licence and technical data: information needed to activate, license, update and support a deployment, such as licence identifiers, software version and basic diagnostic information.
  • Support communications: messages and any files or logs you choose to share with us.
  • Website data: standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested.

5. Security testing data

Security testing data is the most sensitive data connected with the Platform. It includes target lists and scope, credentials and secrets used in testing, tool and agent output, logs, artifacts, findings, proof-of-concept details and reports.

This data belongs to the Customer. Where it reaches Radar 36 at all, we process it only to provide the service or support the Customer has asked for. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

6. AI features

The Platform includes AI features that validate findings, draft their details and answer questions about a project. To do this, relevant project content is sent to an AI model. Depending on how the deployment is configured, that model is one the Customer connects with its own account and keys, or one that Radar 36 makes available.

Where the Customer connects its own AI provider, that provider processes the data under the Customer’s agreement with it. Third-party AI agents that a Customer adds to a workflow are operated under their publishers’ terms, and the Customer decides what data they receive.

7. No sale of data

Radar 36 Inc. does not sell, rent or trade personal data or Customer security data to any third party, advertiser or data broker.

We may use aggregated information that does not identify any person, Customer or target to understand how the Platform performs and to improve it.

8. Third-party tools and the marketplace

The Platform lets Customers install and run security tools, AI agents and integrations made by other publishers, and send results to services such as issue trackers and messaging tools. When a Customer uses one of these, any data passed to it is handled by that publisher under its own terms and privacy policy. Radar 36 does not control how those third parties handle data.

9. Security controls

We design the Platform to keep sensitive testing data contained:

  • Isolated execution: each workflow runs in a dedicated sandbox that is discarded afterwards.
  • Separation by project: the data and credentials of one project are kept apart from those of another.
  • Customer-controlled environments: runs execute on the Customer’s own machines or on isolated cloud virtual machines.
  • A record of every run: who started it, where it ran and what it produced.
  • Encryption in transit: our website and any hosted services are served over HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. In a self-hosted deployment, the security of the underlying infrastructure is the Customer’s responsibility.

10. Service providers

We use trusted service providers to run our business, such as providers of cloud infrastructure, AI models, email delivery and demo scheduling. They process data only as needed to provide their services to us and under agreements that require them to protect it. A current list of sub-processors for hosted services is available on request.

11. Retention and deletion

We keep business contact and account data for as long as we have a relationship with you, and afterwards as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Support files are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request.

Data in a self-hosted deployment is retained and deleted under the Customer’s control. For hosted services, Customers can export their data when a subscription ends, after which we delete it, including from backups, within 60 days unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

12. International transfers and your rights

Radar 36 Inc. is based in the United States, and data we hold may be processed in the United States and other countries. Where required, we protect international transfers of personal data with appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, or to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, contact us using the details below. If your data was put into the Platform by one of our Customers, please contact that Customer, and we will support them in responding.

13. Children

Our website and the Platform are intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

14. Updates and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the law or in the Platform. We will give notice of material changes by email or in the Platform.
