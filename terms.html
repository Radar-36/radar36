2. What the Platform is

Radar 36 is a security orchestration platform. It gives security teams one workspace to:

  • install and run security tools, custom scripts and AI agents, on their own or chained into workflows;
  • execute those runs in isolated sandboxes on the Customer’s own machines or on isolated cloud virtual machines;
  • follow runs with live progress, logs and artifacts;
  • consolidate findings, with AI assistance to validate them and draft their details;
  • share workflows within an organization and obtain tools, agents and workflows from a marketplace.

Radar 36 provides software only. We do not perform penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, security auditing or remediation for you or for your clients. Every test run through the Platform is designed, authorized, started and reviewed by you.
